## Closest prior work (so no novelty claim is made)

| Paper | Read | What it does | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [CRISTAL: Real-time Camera Registration in Static LiDAR Scans using Neural Rendering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16349) (2025) | HTML full text | Renders synthetic views from a coloured LiDAR cloud (z-buffer, colour accumulation within depth threshold delta of the nearest point, hierarchical depth filter against background leaking into the foreground, U-Net hole filling); XFeat matching; PnP-RANSAC, then **refinement by re-rendering at the coarse pose**; "Prebuild & Localize" variant renders a uniform position grid with two pitched views per direction | Same render-then-match idea as M1; evidence on rendering quality and refinement |

## Concrete suggestions, mapped to TODO

1. **Render-and-refine** (CRISTAL): after the PnP pose, re-render at that pose and re-match to remove render-provenance error. Our M1-LoFTR median error is 6.8 cm vs 4.6 cm for M0 (3c). -> TODO A28.
2. **Depth filter against background leakage** (CRISTAL): our renders show far points through gaps in sparse near surfaces (grey halos at skylines in the early render comparison). Cheap GPU min-pooled-depth test. -> A29.
3. **Retrieval before matching** (Zhang et al.; hierarchical localization literature, e.g. [Sarlin et al., From Coarse to Fine, CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/supplemental/Sarlin_From_Coarse_to_CVPR_2019_supplemental.pdf) (supplement only was in the results)): a global descriptor shortlist replaces matching against every render; the literature also notes re-ranking with local matching costs latency, which is where our 3-6 s per query comes from. Candidates: pretrained DINOv2 pooled descriptors (AnyLoc-style, no fine-tuning) or MixVPR/NetVLAD. Needs a model download (ask first). -> A27 (existing).
4. **Lighter matcher** (CRISTAL uses XFeat for real-time): check whether Kornia ships an XFeat implementation/weights before assuming it. -> A30.
5. **Geometry-driven viewpoint sampling for the urban map** (both papers): positions on a ground-level grid in free space, several yaws and slight pitch up/down, instead of the drone's mapping poses. This is TODO C-viewpoint; CRISTAL's two-pitch trick is a cheap addition.
6. **Density sensitivity** (CRISTAL): point-based rendering collapsed at 10 % decimation (24.3 deg error vs 0.65 deg for the neural renderer). Our exp 6 (map degradation) should include downsampling and test a learned hole-filler. -> A31.

## Recording-order issue (already handled)

[ROS Answers: order of timestamps in rosbag2](https://answers.ros.org/question/414352/order-time-stamps-of-messages-in-ros2bag/) confirms that bags are ordered by **receive time**, not header stamp, and that sorting by header stamp in post-processing is the remedy: exactly what `ros/resort_bag.py` and the stamp-sorted image dump do. Separate advice for genuine message loss (recorder QoS overrides with reliable/keep_all/volatile, a larger cache) is not needed here: recorded `/clock` counts match 250 Hz, so messages were late, not lost.
